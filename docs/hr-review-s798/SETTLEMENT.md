The final month runs through `computePayslip` with the end date set to the last day: attendance and OT are cut at that day, SSF is on basic × paid fraction and capped, and the S791 outside-employment drop matches `hr_ot_on_file`'s start day. `hr_ot_on_file` reproduces the engine exactly (rate, sheet OT withheld on days an approved entry covers, NULL `bs_day` entries, two separately rounded parts, Rs 2 tolerance). ## Owner questions from this area
1. **Correcting a settlement that was already paid (SETTLEMENT-1).** When you reopen a paid settlement, fix it and finalize again, Crest still shows the old payment as Paid and gives you no way to pay or record the difference. What should it do? (a) Keep the first payment on record and show "NPR X still to pay" (or "NPR X overpaid") until you record a top-up payment; (b) clear the paid mark on Reopen so you record the whole payment again (simpler, but the record of what went out first is lost); (c) not allow Reopen once a settlement is marked paid.
