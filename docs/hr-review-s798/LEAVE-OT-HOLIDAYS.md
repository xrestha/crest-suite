## Owner questions from this area
1. **Should a manager be able to mark their own attendance? (LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS-5; the same decision as BONUS-LEDGERS owner question 1 and PEOPLE-ACCESS finding 3.)** Today your HR supervisor cannot approve their own leave, but they can open the Attendance Sheet and mark those same days Present or Paid Leave on their own row, and payroll pays from the sheet. Options: (a) they can still mark their own days, but before you finalize payroll you are shown every day a manager marked on their own row, so you can check it (recommended: daily marking stays easy in a small team, and a blocked row would otherwise break the sheet's Save and All Present buttons for them); (b) no: only another manager or you can mark a manager's own days, and the sheet skips their row in every bulk button and says so; (c) leave it as it is, trusting managers with their own attendance. LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS-2 (editing your own approved overtime) and LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS-3 (approving your own leave) need no decision: they complete the rule you already set in S752.
