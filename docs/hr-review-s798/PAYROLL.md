### PAYROLL-1 [P2] Staff set to Inactive, Resigned or Terminated with no last working day are left off that month's payroll, so the days they worked are never paid
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollData.js:374-376` (`fetchPayrollEmployees`); `src/modules/hr/employees/EmployeeForm.jsx:230` (Deactivate writes the status only), `:438` (End Date is hidden for a permanent employee with no date set), `:456-466` (the status select); `src/modules/hr/payroll/PayrollRun.jsx:55-59` (the `assessDraft` comment), `:948-952` and `:1300-1306` (the "departed" copy).
- What happens: Ram is a permanent waiter on NPR 15,000 a month. He works 1–18 Bhadra and then walks out. On 20 Bhadra the manager presses Deactivate or sets his Status to Resigned. There is no box for a last working day, because End Date does not render for a permanent employee who has no date. On 1 Ashwin, Bhadra payroll is generated. Ram has no payslip and nothing names him, so his 18 marked days (about NPR 8,700) pay nothing. If he was deactivated after Generate, Finalize blocks with "not employed in Bhadra". Regenerate's confirm then says "not employed this month — that payslip will be removed", and the manager follows it.
- Evidence: `.or('status.in.(active,probation),end_date.gte.${start}')` drops any off-payroll status whose `end_date` is NULL. Deactivate is `scopedUpdate('hr_employees', { status: 'inactive' })`. The `assessDraft` comment says the list "keeps a leaver on the list to their last day". That holds only when an end date is on the record. Nothing compares the month's `hr_attendance` rows (read at `PayrollRun.jsx:236`) with the payroll list.
- Status: the S751 fix is incomplete. `CHANGELOG/S725-S774.md:261-262` lists "a resigned waiter with no settlement was paid nothing" as fixed by `fetchPayrollEmployees`, but the fix works only when an end date is recorded. Two warnings already tell the manager to "run Final Settlement first": the Deactivate confirm (`EmployeeForm.jsx:219-224`) and the status note (`:463-466`). So this path needs one of those warnings ignored. The money can be recovered if someone notices: Activate, then Final Settlement pays the month, because `hr_pay_month_guard` admits a leaver with no payslip whose settlement is still a draft.
- Fix: in `assessDraft` and on the Generate card, name everyone off the list and not settled who has attendance or approved OT rows in the month ("Ram has 18 marked days in Bhadra but is Inactive with no last working day"), and block Finalize as for `missing`. Split the departed copy by reason (settled, dates outside the month, off-payroll status with no end date), so Regenerate never calls someone with marked days "not employed". What the manager is then told to do depends on the owner question below. Either show End Date for every off-payroll status so a last working day can be entered, or send them to Activate and then Final Settlement.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections (status changed to an incomplete S751 fix; the hidden End Date, the two warnings and the recovery path added); judge confirmed, corrections applied above.

### PAYROLL-3 [P2] An HR manager can move a finalized payroll run to another month over REST, which unlocks the original month for a second run, so its salaries can be recorded and marked paid twice
- Where: LIVE `hr_payroll_runs_guard_settled` fires only `BEFORE UPDATE OF status`. There is no INSERT trigger, and nothing guards `period_id` or `finalized_at`. The RESTRICTIVE insert and update policies admit an HR manager.
- What happens: an HR manager PATCHes the finalized Bhadra run's `period_id`, using their own session token, to a `monthly_periods` row that has no run, such as an IMS month from before HR went live. The payslips, the payments and the month lock all move with the run. hr_payslips has no month of its own, and both `hr_pay_month_guard` and the UNIQUE (client_id, period_id) index key on the run's period. Bhadra is now open. Attendance unlocks, and a new run can be generated and finalized, because `finalize_payroll_run` checks settled leavers, repayments and TADA, never another run's payslips for the same people. Mark paid then records Bhadra's salaries a second time. Separately, an INSERT with status `finalized` creates an empty finalized run. That locks the month's attendance and overtime and refuses every payslip until someone reopens it.
- Evidence: live pg_trigger on hr_payroll_runs lists exactly `audit_hr_payroll_runs`, `hr_payroll_runs_guard_delete` (BEFORE DELETE) and `hr_payroll_runs_guard_settled` (BEFORE UPDATE OF status). The guard body refuses only a status change across `finalized`, for `current_user IN ('anon','authenticated')` and not the operator. information_schema shows no period column on hr_payslips. The only app write to the table is Generate's `scopedInsert('hr_payroll_runs', { period_id, status: 'draft' })` (`PayrollRun.jsx:366`). client_id cannot move to another tenant, because the `client_own` WITH CHECK requires `my_client_id()`.
- Status: missed by S791. Its rule says "a run's status never crosses finalized except through Finalize/Reopen", and moving the period gets round that without touching the status. It also defeats the intent of `hr_payroll_runs_guard_delete`, that a finalized run cannot leave its month.
- Fix: make the guard BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE with no column list, using the seam it already has (`current_user IN ('anon','authenticated') AND NOT COALESCE(is_admin(), false)`, so the DEFINER Finalize/Reopen and an operator restore pass). Refuse an INSERT with status other than `draft`. Refuse any change to `period_id`, draft or finalized, since no app path changes it. Refuse a direct change to `finalized_at`. Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed against the live catalog. Exploiting it needs an HR manager replaying their JWT, and every change is audited. Not executed.
- Verified: the reviewer graded P3. The verifier confirmed and raised it to P2 after finding that the period move frees the original month for a second payroll. The judge re-read the live trigger set and found no app write of `period_id`, so the fix now refuses every period change rather than only a finalized run's.

## Owner questions from this area
1. **Staff taken off payroll mid-month without a last working day (PAYROLL-1).** Take a waiter who walks out and is set to Inactive or Resigned without a last working day, but has days marked on this month's attendance. Payroll leaves him out, and the days he worked are never paid. Nothing on the Payroll page names him. What should payroll do? (a) Stop and name him until you either enter his last working day or run his Final Settlement. The employee form would then show the last-working-day box for anyone set to Inactive, Resigned or Terminated. (b) Pay him automatically up to his last marked day. (c) Leave him out, but show his name and marked days as a warning. Recommended: (a). You decide the last day, nothing is guessed, and someone who walked out is still paid for the days he worked.
