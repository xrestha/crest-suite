### SISTER-1 [P2] An HR supervisor can cancel their own leave after it was approved and taken, which puts the days back on their balance to be encashed at exit; over REST they can also delete or re-date it with no guard
- Where: `src/modules/hr/leave/LeaveManagement.jsx:766-768` (Cancel on every pending or approved row, the viewer's own included), `:418-443` decideRequest and `:446-467` decideRequestNow (revertAttendance `:256-278`, then status 'cancelled'); live `hr_leave_requests_guard_decision` (own-record refusal only when `NEW.status IN ('approved','rejected')`; an update with the status unchanged returns early); live triggers on hr_leave_requests are audit, guard_decision and validate, none on DELETE; live RESTRICTIVE `hr_leave_requests_write_rank_update` / `_delete` (admin, Owner or HR supervisor/manager, no status or own-record test); `src/modules/hr/leave/leaveBalance.js:28-35` (leaveUsed counts approved rows only); `src/modules/hr/settlement/FinalSettlement.jsx:305-312` (encashment pre-fill).
- What happens: Ramesh, the floor manager, has an HR Supervisor login linked to his employee record. The Owner approves his 10 days of paid Annual Leave in Kartik and he takes them. Before Kartik's payroll is finalized he presses Cancel on his own approved row. The confirm says "those days go back to unmarked and the leave balance is restored". The marks are deleted (a blank day is paid for monthly staff, so his pay does not move) and the request reads Cancelled. His balance is back to what he has earned to date, as if he never took the 10 days. If he leaves in the same BS year, Final Settlement pre-fills 10 more days to encash: 10 x 30,000 / 26 = about NPR 11,540 on a 30,000 basic, finalized by an Owner or HR manager with no reason to doubt the pre-fill. Only the audit log shows who cancelled; the Leave page does not show decided_by. No app path deletes an hr_leave_requests row except the service-role client wipe (admin-user-ops/index.ts:240, :2103), so a pending-only delete rule breaks nothing. hss: 72faa01, migration 20260925000008:38-39 and :377-420 (a decided request keeps its dates, days, type and employee; only a pending request may be deleted); BUILD-LOG 10054-10057 (#4) and 10198-10200 (#43).
