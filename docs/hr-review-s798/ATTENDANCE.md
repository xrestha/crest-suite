1. **A working shift with no length set (ATTENDANCE-2).** The ready-made "Split" shift comes with no hours. Today, when someone works it, Attendance counts every hour as overtime, and Generate from Roster marks the day Off, so a daily-wage worker is paid nothing for it. What should Attendance assume for such a day? (a) An ordinary 8-hour day: overtime only beyond 8 hours, Generate marks the day Present with 8 hours, and the sheet shows a note asking you to set the shift's hours. (b) Work out the hours from the times but leave overtime blank for the manager to type, and Generate leaves the day blank. (c) Calculate nothing, and refuse Generate, until the shift's hours are set. Recommended: (a), because that is already how a day with no roster entry works.
2. **Generate from Roster filling days that have not happened yet (ATTENDANCE-5).** Today it fills the whole month from the roster, so pressing it on the 12th marks the 13th to the 30th Present in advance, and those days stay Present unless someone changes them. Should it (a) fill only up to today, the way Import from machine already works, so you run it again later in the month; (b) keep filling the whole month but say in the confirm how many of the days are still in the future; or (c) stay as it is? Recommended: (a). In every option, days before someone joined or after their last day would be skipped.
3. **Staff marked Present who never punched in (ATTENDANCE-4).** If you filled the sheet with Generate from Roster or All Present and then import the fingerprint file, a person who did not come in keeps their Present mark and is paid for the day, and the import does not mention it. Should the import (a) list those days in its review ("marked Present, no fingerprint, rostered to work") with a tick, off by default, to mark them Absent; (b) list them for information only; or (c) stay as it is? Recommended: (a). Nothing already marked changes unless you tick it, which keeps the rule that the import never overwrites your marks by itself.
