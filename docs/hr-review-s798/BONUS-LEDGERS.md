### BONUS-LEDGERS-1 [P1] An HR manager can make their own loan or advance go unrecovered: delete it, record a cash repayment that never happened, or move it onto a colleague. Only the write-off is refused
- Where: live trigger `hr_advances_guard_own` fires BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE only, and its body tests only `hr_is_own_employee(NEW.employee_id)`, then the write-off, `amount` and `employee_id` (`supabase/migrations/20260914230000_hr_staff_settlement_gratuity.sql:188-212`, identical live). Live `hr_advance_repayments` has no own-record trigger, only audit, guard, guard_ledger and sync_status. `src/modules/hr/advances/Advances.jsx:318-346` (handleRepay), `:410-433` (handleDelete) and `:624-658` (Record Repayment and Delete). The file has no own-record test anywhere.
- What happens: Sita is the restaurant's HR manager, on NPR 40,000 basic, and her login is linked to her employee record. The Owner issues her a loan of NPR 60,000 at NPR 5,000 a month on 20 Bhadra. Any one of these works, and none of them is flagged on any payroll or advance screen, though the audit log records who did it. (a) Before the Ashwin payroll takes the first cut, she selects the loan and presses Delete. Nothing refuses, so no payroll ever cuts the NPR 60,000. (b) After two cuts, she presses "+ Record Repayment", types NPR 50,000 with the note "cash", and the status trigger marks the loan Settled. The ledger now shows a cash repayment of NPR 50,000 that never happened. (c) Before the first cut, she moves the loan onto a colleague over REST. The guard tests only the new employee, so it passes, and the colleague's salary is cut for her loan. (d) Over REST she sets `installment_amount` to 0.01 or `issued_date` to 2090-01-01. Payroll then cuts one paisa a month, or nothing. If she presses "Write off NPR 50,000" instead, the database refuses with `hr_own_request`.
- Evidence: S752 (hr-payroll.md:689-691, and its code comment: "a manager could issue themselves an advance and write it off") is the rule this bypasses. Live `hr_advances_guard` refuses a DELETE only when repayments exist. `hr_advance_repayments_guard` checks the client, the employee, active status and the owed cap. `guard_ledger` refuses only rows tagged `payroll_run_id` or `final_settlement_id`. The CHECK `hr_advances_installment_check` allows any value above 0, and `issued_date` has no check. payrollData.js:229/265/305 cut `installment_amount` from `firstRecoveryMonth(issued_date)`. The restrictive write policies on both tables admit admin, the Owner or `hr_role = 'manager'`. errorText.js:744 already words `hr_own_request`.
- Status: missed by S752. Its own-record fence covered the write-off, amount and employee only, and tested only the new row.
- Fix: extend `hr_advances_guard_own` to BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE. On UPDATE, test `hr_is_own_employee(OLD.employee_id) OR hr_is_own_employee(NEW.employee_id)`, and refuse a change to `installment_amount`, `issued_date` or `type` as well as `amount` and `employee_id`. On DELETE, test OLD and refuse. Add `hr_advance_repayments_guard_own` (BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE): it refuses when the caller is not `hr_self_decision_exempt()` and the advance belongs to them. Keep the `current_user` early return: finalize_payroll_run, finalize_final_settlement, reopen_payroll_run and reopen_final_settlement are all SECURITY DEFINER live, so they keep writing. On the page, an `isOwnAdvance` test, like TadaClaims' `isOwnClaim`, hides Record Repayment and Delete on your own advance with "Someone else must record this". The fence reaches only logins linked to their employee record (SISTER review finding 1). Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed from the live catalog (trigger list and both guard bodies read by the judge). Not exercised with a live write.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections (path (c), moving the advance to a colleague, and the OLD/NEW test in the fix have been added above); judge confirmed at P1. ### BONUS-LEDGERS-4 [P2] A leaver whose last month is also a bonus's pay month can be under-taxed, because Final Settlement's year leaves out a festival allowance or incentive paid that month
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollData.js:71` (the bonus loop drops a bonus whose `monthInFy >= cur.monthInFy`); `src/modules/hr/settlement/FinalSettlement.jsx:257` (`fetchYtdMap(scopedFrom, period)`, where period is the last month), `:316` (festivalAlreadyPaid sets festival_pro to 0); `src/modules/hr/settlement/settlementCompute.js:97-110` (the final-month true-up from that YTD), `:142` (lumpTds on top of `finalTax.annualTaxable`).
- What happens: a waiter-turned-supervisor, not in SSF, earns NPR 80,000 a month (basic 60,000). Monthly TDS projects 9.6 lakh, so Shrawan and Bhadra withhold NPR 800 each at the 1% band. The Dashain run pays him NPR 60,000 in Ashwin and withholds NPR 2,400 (the last 20,000 crosses into 10%). He resigns with 30 Ashwin as his last day, and his settlement adds about NPR 8 lakh in gratuity and leave encashment. Final Settlement reads the year with period = Ashwin, so the Ashwin Dashain row is skipped. The year is trued up on 2.4 lakh of salary (NPR 800 more), and the 8-lakh lump sum is taxed on top of 2.4 lakh instead of 3.0 lakh: NPR 11,600. Total withheld: NPR 16,400. His FY 2083/84 income is 11 lakh, on which NPR 20,000 is due. The NPR 3,600 shortfall is stored in `lump_tds`, and the employer carries it. An incentive run paid in the leaver's last month has the same effect. The error is bounded by roughly the same-month bonus times the rate gap at the band it crosses. It appears only when earlier withholding did not already overshoot: a bonus taxed before the resignation, while no end date was on file, projects a full year and usually over-withholds.
- Evidence: payrollData.js:67-76. Dropping the same-month bonus is right for a monthly payslip, where that bonus carried its own marginal tax. It is wrong for a settlement, which trues the year up to actual income (hr-payroll.md S752) and is the leaver's last chance. SLABS_2083_84 (tds.js:21-27): 1% up to 10 lakh, then 10%.
- Status: missed by S752. The settlement's tax was built on fetchYtdMap's earlier-months cut.
- Fix: give `ytdFromPayslips` an option such as `{ includeSameMonth: true }` and use it only from FinalSettlement, so the true-up and the lump-sum base both include a finalized bonus paid in the final month. Keep the bonus's own tax in `bonusWithheld`, so the settled-at-source split stays consistent. Folding a finalized settlement into the bonus side (`payslipYtdForFy`) is ENGINE-5's fix. Do it there, not twice. Optional polish: mark a settled leaver on Incentive Run ("Settled on <date>") as Festival Allowance does, so the manager knows their tax is worked out apart from the settlement.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading. Figures worked by hand from the FY 2083/84 slabs.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections, which are applied above. ## Owner questions from this area
1. **Should an HR manager be able to set their own pay? (BONUS-LEDGERS-5, together with PEOPLE-ACCESS finding 3 and LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS finding 4.)** Today your HR manager cannot approve their own leave or write off their own loan, but they can type their own Dashain allowance or bonus, raise their own basic salary, or mark their own absences as present, then finalize the run that pays them. Options: (a) they can prepare any run, but you must finalize one that pays them, the way you already finalize their own settlement (recommended: one rule, and it cannot be dodged by changing the salary first); (b) they can finalize, but the Finalize confirmation names every row where they changed their own figures, and you see it afterwards; (c) leave it as now, trusting the HR manager with pay. BONUS-LEDGERS-1 (deleting or faking repayment of your own loan) needs no decision: it completes the rule you already set in S752.
2. **When a travel claim is approved by mistake, what should a manager be able to do before payroll pays it? (BONUS-LEDGERS-2.)** Example: a rider sends a claim, then a corrected copy, and both are approved together, so payroll will pay both. Options: (a) an "Undo approval" that puts the claim back to Pending, for an HR manager or you only, while no finalized payroll has paid it (recommended; Leave and Overtime already work this way); (b) allow rejecting an approved claim directly, with no step back through Pending; (c) keep it as now: once approved it is paid, and mistakes are fixed by hand outside Crest.
