### PEOPLE-ACCESS-1 [P0] Any HR supervisor can change an employee's basic salary, bank account, SSF registration, status and dates, or add and delete employees, over REST, and the next payroll and bank sheet pay from the changed record
- Where: live `hr_employees` policies. The permissive `hr_employees_insert/update/delete` are `(is_admin() OR client_id = my_client_id()) AND NOT is_hr_self_service()`, and the only restrictive ones are `no_hr_staff_rank`, `no_ims_staff` and `no_pos_pin_staff`. The S751 write fence (`supabase/migrations/20260914210000_hr_payroll_money_pages.sql:27-30`) covers nine money tables and leaves out `hr_employees`.
- What happens: Ram, the head chef, runs the roster and attendance with an HR supervisor login linked to his own employee record. With his own session token he sends `PATCH /rest/v1/hr_employees?id=eq.<his id>` with `{basic_salary: 45000}` (it was 30,000). The database accepts it. The manager's next Generate reads the live basic, so Ram is paid NPR 15,000 more every month, and his festival share and gratuity grow with it. Nothing on Payroll or the approval sheet marks a pay change; only the Audit Log records it. With the same kind of request he can:
  - change a coworker's `bank_account_no`, so the HR Reports bank sheet sends that coworker's net pay to Ram's account;
  - set someone to terminated or give them a past `end_date`, which takes them off payroll;
  - change `join_date`, which moves gratuity and pro-rating;
  - change `ssf_enrolled` / `ssf_no`, which switches SSF and the 1% SST waiver;
  - set `access_blocked` back to false on a blocked leaver, so their Self-Service PIN works again;
  - INSERT an active "ghost" employee, who is paid at the next run;
  - DELETE a new hire with no pay history, which takes their attendance and leave with them.
  If Ram's login is matched to his record only by email (the fallback in `hr_is_own_employee`), he can also change the email on his employee row. The own-record guards then stop recognising him, and he can approve his own overtime.
- Evidence: live `is_hr_staff_rank()` is `COALESCE(hr_role = 'staff', false)`; `is_ims_staff()` is `ims_role IS NOT NULL`; `is_pos_pin_staff()` is `pos_email IS NOT NULL`. None of them catches an HR supervisor. The live triggers on `hr_employees` are only `audit_hr_employees`, `hr_employees_guard_delete` (pay-history delete guard) and `hr_employees_revoke_self_service_sessions`. There is no rank or column guard. `authenticated` holds INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE and column UPDATE on `basic_salary` and `bank_account_no`. Compare `hr_salary_components`, which carries `hr_salary_components_write_rank_*`. The S750 decision (`20260914200000:10-12`) was that supervisors keep reading pay, and its live probe tested only a staff-rank update and a supervisor read (`CHANGELOG/S725-S774.md:394-396`). Payroll reads basic, pay basis, SSF, status, join and end dates live (`payrollData.js:351`, `:374`), and the bank sheet reads `bank_account_no` live (`HrReports.jsx:119`, `:829`). Writer inventory: every app writer is on a manager-only page (`EmployeeForm.jsx:197/201/230/241/310`, `EmployeeList.jsx:255`, `PayForm.jsx:121`; `Layout.js:207-208`, `EmployeeList.jsx:341`, `PaySetup.jsx:139`). The only live function that writes the table is `finalize_final_settlement` (SECURITY DEFINER; `relforcerowsecurity` is false).
- Status: missed by S751. Its money-table write fence left out the table that holds basic salary, bank account and SSF registration, although its own reason ("every page that writes them is manager-only") applies here too. Nothing in the rules, the CHANGELOG or CROSS-REPO records supervisor writes to `hr_employees` as a decision.
- Fix: add RESTRICTIVE `hr_employees_write_rank_insert/update/delete` TO authenticated with the S751 `rank_ok` expression (`COALESCE(is_admin() OR is_client_owner() OR caller hr_role = 'manager', false)`). Leave reads open to supervisors, per S750. The writer inventory shows this refuses no legitimate path: Final Settlement is DEFINER, restore runs as admin, and Danger Zone uses the service role. Prove it with a rolled-back probe: a supervisor PATCH of `basic_salary` changes 0 rows, a supervisor SELECT still returns the row, and a manager PATCH works. In the probe, strip the stand-in's other staff markers so a restrictive policy cannot make the result vacuous. Add `hr_employees` to the money-table list in `supabase-sql.md`. A refused RLS update is 0 rows with no error, so have PayForm and EmployeeForm `.select('id')` and treat 0 rows as not saved. Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed on the live catalog (policies, helper bodies, triggers, grants and the writer scan). Not exercised with a live write. Exploiting it needs an insider replaying their own session token against /rest/v1.
- Verified: verifier confirmed at P0. The judge re-read the live policies, the four helper bodies, the triggers and a fresh pg_proc writer scan, and found no guard that either had missed. Confirmed at P0.

### PEOPLE-ACCESS-2 [P2] An HR manager can raise their own salary or add themselves an allowance in Pay Setup, then generate and finalize the payroll that pays it
- Where: `src/modules/hr/pay/PayForm.jsx:109-157` (`handleSave` updates the `hr_employees` pay, bank and SSF columns, then deletes and re-inserts `hr_salary_components`, with no own-record check). Live triggers: `hr_employees` has audit, delete guard and session revoke only; `hr_salary_components` has audit only. Rule: `.claude/rules/hr-payroll.md:689-691`.
- What happens: Sita keeps the books and holds an HR manager login linked to her own employee record. In Pay Setup she opens her own row and raises basic from 35,000 to 45,000, or adds "Special Allowance 8,000". She then generates and finalizes the month herself. She is paid NPR 8,000 to 10,000 more every month, plus employer SSF on any basic rise. The database would refuse her approving her own leave or overtime, issuing or writing off her own advance, and finalizing her own settlement. This change is larger than any of those, and it is accepted. Nothing on Payroll or the approval sheet compares pay with last month, so only the Audit Log shows it.
- Evidence: the `hr_salary_components` write-rank policies admit any `hr_role = 'manager'`. Live `hr_is_own_employee` (profile link, or employee email = JWT email) already backs `hr_overtime_guard_own`, `hr_advances_guard_own`, the leave decision guard and `finalize/reopen_final_settlement`. The S752 rule "Nobody below the Owner decides their own record" names leave, overtime and advances only (settlements since S753). Pay Setup is not mentioned in it or in any S752/S753 entry, so this case was never considered rather than decided. `PayrollApprovalSheet.jsx` has no month-over-month pay comparison.
- Status: missed by S752. BONUS-LEDGERS-5 is the festival and bonus version of this gap, and LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS-5 is the attendance version. (Earlier drafts, including BONUS-LEDGERS' owner question 1, call this finding "PEOPLE-ACCESS finding 3"; it is PEOPLE-ACCESS-2.)
- Fix: depends on Owner question 1. For the Pay Setup fence, add a BEFORE UPDATE trigger on `hr_employees` that refuses a change to `basic_salary`, `pay_basis`, `ssf_enrolled`, `ssf_no`, `bank_name`, `bank_account_no`, `bank_branch` or the premiums when `NOT hr_self_decision_exempt() AND hr_is_own_employee(NEW.id)` (hint `hr_own_request`). Add a BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE trigger on `hr_salary_components` that applies the same test to `employee_id`. Keep a `current_user` early return for DEFINER and restore paths. In PayForm, open the caller's own row read-only with "The Owner sets your own pay". Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading and on the live catalog. The HR Manager rank is granted only by the Owner or admin, and the Audit Log records the change.
- Verified: verifier confirmed at P2, and noted that a Pay Setup fence alone does not stop a manager finalizing their own payslip, so it should be decided together with BONUS-LEDGERS-5. Judge confirmed at P2.

### PEOPLE-ACCESS-3 [P2] Taking back a settled leaver the way the refusal and Help describe leaves their old End Date on the record, and Payroll then leaves them out of every month without a word
- Where: `src/modules/hr/employees/EmployeeForm.jsx:144-155` (the rehire refusal), `:184-199` (the save accepts `end_date` before `join_date`), `:449-452` (an advisory warning only); `employeeFormData.js:86-92`; `src/pages/Help.js:349`; `src/pages/settings/hrGuideData.js:634`; `payrollCompute.js:338-344` (`employedInPeriod`); `payrollData.js:371-395`.
- What happens: Hari (monthly, NPR 22,000) was settled with last working day 15 Ashadh 2083. Finalize set him to resigned and set his End Date to that day. The owner takes him back on 1 Kartik. Setting him Active is refused with "set a Join Date after that day — the rehire is a new employment and is paid from then", and Help says the same. The manager sets Join Date 1 Kartik and saves. The End Date still says 15 Ashadh. The form does show an amber warning under the dates ("This end date has passed, but Hari is still on payroll — payroll pays them nothing after it. Clear the date if they are still working."). But the save goes through, and the refusal and Help both describe the rehire as done once the join date moves. `fetchPayrollEmployees` drops anyone whose end date is before the month starts, so Hari is missing from Kartik's payroll and every month after. Payroll lists him neither as settled nor as left out. He is simply not there.
- Evidence: live `finalize_final_settlement` runs `UPDATE hr_employees SET status = v_new_status, end_date = s.last_working_date, access_blocked = true`. `rehireNeedsNewJoinDate` returns null once join is after the settled last day (`employeeFormData.js:91`), and `handleSave` writes the diff with the old `end_date` untouched. `employedInPeriod`: `if (end && end < periodStartAd) return false`. The S791 CHANGELOG never pairs the rehire with the End Date.
- Status: missed by S791.
- Fix: page only. When a settled leaver is being put back on payroll (the `rehireProblem` path), either refuse with "Clear the End Date — it is the last day of their earlier employment" if the End Date is on or before the settled last day or before the new join date, or clear it in the same save and say so. In general, refuse any save where `end_date < join_date`. Name both steps in the refusal text, the Help rehire tip (Help.js:349) and hrGuideData.js:634.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading. Nobody is paid wrong and nothing wrong is stored. The rehired person is missing from the run, and someone will probably notice on payday.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections: the Employees form does show the amber warning on the tab the refusal opens, and Help's End Date tip names it, so the silence is on Payroll, not on the form. Judge confirmed at P2; corrections applied above.

### PEOPLE-ACCESS-4 [P2] A settled leaver who is rehired cannot get their old till, stock or HR login back except by reopening the settlement, and the staff pages refuse to create a second login for them
- Where: live `finalize_final_settlement` (bans every login linked by `hr_employee_id` and stamps `settlement_blocked_by`); live `reopen_final_settlement` (the only unban); `EmployeeForm.jsx:144-155` and `employeeFormData.js:86-92` (the S791 rehire path touches no login); `supabase/functions/admin-user-ops/index.ts:1011-1013` (POS), `1588-1591` and `1651-1654` (IMS), `1867-1869` (HR).
- What happens: Sunita, a waiter with a POS PIN login linked to her employee record, leaves in Ashadh and is settled. Finalize bans her login. She comes back for Dashain, and the manager follows S791: new join date after her last day, Active. Payroll pays her, but she is missing from the till's staff picker. POS Staff shows her login with a "Blocked at settlement" badge, and Reset PIN cannot unblock it. Creating a new linked login is refused ("This employee already has a POS staff account"), and so are the HR and IMS equivalents. The manager has three ways out, and each does harm:
  - reopen the settlement, which un-settles the old employment;
  - create an unlinked login, which the next settlement will not find or block;
  - delete the old login, the harm S753 avoided by blocking rather than deleting: every bill, KOT and shift she recorded loses her name (ON DELETE SET NULL).
  A rehired HR supervisor is in the same position, and IMS Staff and HR Staff show no badge at all, so a manager there cannot see why the login fails.
- Evidence: a live pg_proc scan finds `banned_until` only in `finalize_final_settlement` and `reopen_final_settlement`, and grep finds no ban or unban in `supabase/functions`. Reopen clears `banned_until` only for profiles `WHERE settlement_blocked_by = s.id`. Live `get_pos_staff` / `get_pos_device_staff` filter on `settlement_blocked_by IS NULL`. PosStaff.jsx:584-593 renders the badge; ImsStaff.jsx and HrStaff.jsx read no settlement flag. The partial unique index `profiles_hr_employee_pos_unique` also blocks a second linked POS login. The copy (Help.js:349, hrGuideData.js:634) teaches the rehire without mentioning logins. Self-Service is not affected, because Employees' Activate restores `access_blocked`.
- Status: missed by S791. S791 added the rehire path; the S753 decision to block rather than delete predates it and names Reopen as the only unban. This does not challenge S753.
- Fix: depends on Owner question 2. For option (a), add a SECURITY DEFINER `hr_unblock_rehired_logins(p_employee uuid)`. It requires manager rank and the same client, and refuses the caller's own record. It finds the latest finalized settlement whose `last_working_date` is before the employee's current `join_date`, then clears `banned_until` and `settlement_blocked_by` for exactly the profiles that settlement stamped. Call it after a successful rehire save in EmployeeForm, or run it as an AFTER UPDATE OF join_date, status trigger. Reopen then finds nothing left to unban. In every option, show the "Blocked at settlement" badge on IMS Staff and HR Staff as POS Staff already does. Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed on the live catalog. It needs a settled leaver who held a staff login and is rehired.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections: POS Staff already shows the badge, so only IMS Staff and HR Staff lack it; at the till she is missing from the picker rather than failing a PIN. Judge confirmed at P2; corrections applied above.

## Owner questions from this area
1. **Should an HR manager be able to change their own salary, allowances or bank account in Pay Setup? (PEOPLE-ACCESS-2; decide together with BONUS-LEDGERS question 1, which covers LEAVE-OT-HOLIDAYS-5 and BONUS-LEDGERS-5 too.)** Today your HR manager cannot approve their own leave, overtime or advance, but they can raise their own basic salary and then finalize the payroll that pays it. Options:
   - (a) Only you change the HR manager's own pay setup. They still see it, and everyone else's pay stays theirs to set.
   - (b) They can change it, but the payroll approval sheet you sign lists every employee whose pay setup changed since last month, the manager included.
   - (c) Keep it as now, trusting the HR manager with everyone's pay, their own included.

   Recommended: (a), together with option (a) of BONUS-LEDGERS question 1 (you finalize any run that pays them). The Pay Setup fence stops the change where it is made. Your Finalize catches the rest, but only if you notice a changed basic on the payslip.
2. **When you take back someone who left and was settled, should their old Crest logins come back with them? (PEOPLE-ACCESS-4.)** Their till PIN, stock-count login or HR login was blocked at settlement, and today the only way to unblock it is to reopen the old settlement. Options:
   - (a) Yes, automatically when you save their new join date. The same logins the settlement blocked start working again.
   - (b) Not automatically. Each blocked login shows "Blocked at settlement" on its staff page, with an Unblock button for you or that module's manager.
   - (c) No. Delete the old login and make a new one, which blanks their name on every bill and shift they recorded before.

   Recommended: (a). It is the same person, and their name stays on their old bills.
