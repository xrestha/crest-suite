## Owner questions from this area
1. **What should the bank transfer sheet list when some staff are already marked paid? (REPORTS-1)** Today it always lists every employee at full net pay. If you reopen a month to add a missed overtime entry, or you paid two helpers in cash, the sheet still lists everyone at their full pay. Uploading it to the bank again pays those people twice. The options:
   - (a) List only what each person is still owed, and leave off anyone already paid, naming them above the table.
   - (b) List everyone at full pay, with a "Paid" column and a warning at the top.
   - (c) Leave it as it is.

   Recommended: (a). The sheet can then never pay someone twice, and the owner can still see who was left off and why.
