## Owner questions from this area

1. **Dashain bonus, incentives and a leaver's final pay in labour cost (LABOUR-FIGURES-1).** When you pay the Dashain allowance, an incentive, or a leaver's final pay, should Crest count it as labour cost?
   - (A) Yes, in the month it is paid, with a line under the figure such as "includes Dashain bonus NPR 2,07,600" so the spike is explained. **Recommended**: it matches what left your bank.
   - (B) Spread the Dashain bonus: count one-twelfth of it every month so no month spikes. Smoother, but each month then carries an estimate of a payment not yet made.
   - (C) Leave them out, but print "bonuses and final pay not included" under every labour figure.
   - Also: a leaver's gratuity builds up over years. Should it count in the month it is paid (simple, matches the bank), or be left out of monthly labour and shown only in HR reports? Recommendation: the month it is paid, named on the line, same as (A).

2. **Joiners and unpaid days (LABOUR-FIGURES-3; this reopens the S756 formula).** When a waiter joins halfway through the month, or takes unpaid days off, should the labour cost on your dashboards count the full month's salary or only what they actually earned?
   - (A) Only what was earned: the same "Cost to business" figure as on the payroll sheet you approve. **Recommended**: one number everywhere, and daily-wage and monthly staff are then treated alike.
   - (B) The full contract salary, and rename the payroll sheet's figure so the two are not read as the same thing.

3. **Present days with no clock times (LABOUR-FIGURES-7).** If you mark staff Present without typing their hours, should the Owner Report assume they worked their rostered shift (and Roster's labour forecast assume the same), or should it just say "hours not recorded" and leave out the hours-based figures? **Recommended**: say "hours not recorded" for now; that fix needs no decision and is shipped either way. Assuming shift hours would change the Roster forecast too.
