Every order of the pay-state writes was traced, and these pairs hold, so the next review can skip them. Payroll Finalize against a manual repayment, write-off or delete of an advance: finalize_payroll_run row-locks each advance and the repayment guard locks the same row, so over-recovery, a write-off after Generate and a deleted advance are all refused, and a reactivated advance makes the draft drift. Payroll Reopen over a write-off is refused (S791). Payroll Finalize and Settlement Finalize, in either order: `settlement_month_paid` and `run_has_settled_employee` both run under `hr_pay_lock`, for the current employment only. Mark paid and Void against a settlement: a live payment for the final month or later refuses the settlement, after which no finalized run can carry that month, and voids keep the row. TADA against payroll and settlement: a claim paid elsewhere while it sits in a draft run refuses that run's Finalize (`payroll_tada_changed`), Regenerate drops it, and each Reopen reverts only the claims it paid; a changed approved set refuses a settlement (`settlement_stale_tada`). Advances against settlement: a leaver's payroll cut followed by a settlement that recovers everything is refused at payroll Finalize, and a payroll reopened after the settlement reactivates only its own cut. Overtime and attendance: `hr_pay_month_guard` (under `hr_pay_lock`) locks a finalized payroll month and a finalized settlement's month and later, the draft-settlement exception is bounded by day, and S791's stale-OT check covers overtime approved after the draft. Festival Reopen discloses the settlement share, and re-Finalize blocks a row "Paid by Final Settlement". Monthly TDS counts only earlier-month bonuses, and a bonus finalized for an earlier month after Generate shows as TDS drift at Finalize. Both Crest Staff submit RPCs assert active access, so a settled leaver cannot submit. Regenerating an earlier month after a settlement reproduces the leaver's payslip, so the settlement's year-to-date stays right unless that month's inputs changed.

### GAP-PAY-STATE-1 [P1] An HR manager can put a travel claim that payroll already paid back to Approved over REST, so the next payroll pays it again. This works on their own claim, and no screen shows it
- Where: live `hr_tada_claims_guard`, the branch commented "Payroll Reopen: only a claim payroll itself paid goes back to Approved" (latest file `supabase/migrations/20260914220000_hr_money_guards_stamp_for_operator.sql:143`; first added in `20260914210000_hr_payroll_money_pages.sql:525`); live `reopen_payroll_run` and `reopen_final_settlement` (SECURITY DEFINER, owner postgres); `src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollData.js:93-100` (fetchApprovedTadaMap); `src/modules/hr/tada/TadaClaims.jsx:450` (the only app status write on a decided claim); `.claude/rules/hr-payroll.md:654`.
- What happens: Sita, the HR manager, is linked to her employee record. She files a NPR 4,800 claim for a vendor trip, another manager approves it, and the Shrawan payroll pays it and marks it Paid (Payroll). Using her own session token, she PATCHes `/rest/v1/hr_tada_claims?id=eq.<claim>` with `status: 'approved'`. The guard lets it through, because it checks only that she is a manager. It does not check whose claim it is or whether the Shrawan run is still finalized. TADA Claims now shows the claim as Approved and due in the next payroll. Bhadra's Generate picks it up and Bhadra's Finalize marks it Paid again, so she has been paid NPR 4,800 twice, and she can repeat this every month. Shrawan's payslip still lists the claim, so a later Reopen of Shrawan also reverts the payment Bhadra made. She can do the same to any colleague's claim. The audit log records the PATCH, but `/admin/audit` is operator-only, so no screen shows it to the tenant.
- Evidence: live body: the first line returns for every `current_user` outside anon/authenticated, and the operator branch follows. For everyone else, `IF OLD.status = 'paid' AND NEW.status = 'approved' AND OLD.paid_method = 'Payroll' THEN IF NOT public.hr_is_manager_rank() THEN RAISE …; NEW.paid_at := NULL; NEW.paid_method := NULL; RETURN NEW`. `hr_is_own_employee` is tested only on pending → approved/rejected. Both Reopen functions are `prosecdef = true`, owned by postgres, so their UPDATEs take the first-line return and never reach this branch. `approved_by` is left as it was, so the claim looks like a normal approved claim. fetchApprovedTadaMap reads `status = 'approved' AND end_date <= month end`. Live `finalize_payroll_run` marks its payslips' claims paid `WHERE status = 'approved'` and never checks whether another finalized payslip already lists the claim. Live `reopen_payroll_run` reverts every claim in its payslips' `tada_claim_ids` that is paid by Payroll. No app code moves paid → approved. The branch came with S751 (a0d7ee9b), when Reopen still ran as browser writes; S753 (fbd871ab) moved Reopen into `reopen_payroll_run` and left the branch in place.
- Status: missed by S753. The S751 branch outlived the browser Reopen it was written for, and BONUS-LEDGERS listed it as sound without asking who still uses it.
- Fix: delete the branch, so paid → approved falls through to `tada_transition_invalid` for every caller that is not the operator. Both Reopen functions and the operator restore path return before the branch, so no page changes. Reword hr-payroll.md:654 to say the transition happens only inside `reopen_payroll_run` / `reopen_final_settlement`. Migration needed.
- Confidence: confirmed from the live catalog (the judge re-read the guard, both payroll function bodies and the fetch). Not exercised with a live write.
- Verified: verifier confirmed at P2; judge confirmed and raised it to P1. BONUS-LEDGERS-1 (P1) is the same kind of defect: it defeats the S752 own-record rule that the database already enforces for this table ("never your own claim"), it pays money that stays paid, and it is invisible to the tenant. The P2 cases the verifier compared it with, BONUS-LEDGERS-5 and PEOPLE-ACCESS-2, are cases the rule never covered, and they need an owner decision. This one needs no decision: the fix removes a dead branch.

### GAP-PAY-STATE-2 [P2] Leave and overtime still awaiting a decision when a Final Settlement is finalized are paid wrongly: pending leave is paid as worked and encashed again, pending overtime is never paid, and neither can be approved afterwards

- What happens: Bikash, a waiter on NPR 26,000 basic, has 20 Poush as his last day. On 14 Poush he asks in Crest Staff for annual leave from 15 to 18 Poush and stays home. The request sits in the queue. A supervisor also records 6 hours of overtime for him on 19 Poush, awaiting a manager. On 21 Poush the manager settles him. The final month pays 1 to 20 Poush in full, because blank days are paid for monthly staff, and the leave box is prefilled with his earned balance as if the 4 days were never taken. Those 4 days are encashed as well: 4 × 26,000 / 26 = NPR 4,000 overpaid. Had the request been for unpaid leave, the 4 days would be paid as worked, about NPR 3,600 overpaid. The overtime is not in the settlement, which counts approved entries only. After Finalize, `hr_month_settled` locks Poush for him. Approving the leave is refused, and Leave says the days "could not be marked … NOT approved". The overtime can be neither approved nor rejected, and its NPR 1,000 or so is paid by nobody unless someone reopens the settlement. Only rejecting the leave still works, because rejecting writes no attendance. The page never mentions pending requests. A paid leave approved after the draft was saved is encashed too, because a saved draft keeps its leave days and Finalize never compares them with the balance.
- Evidence: `grep pending FinalSettlement.jsx` finds 0 hits. leaveUsed filters `r.status === 'approved'`. The overtime read is `.eq('status', 'approved')`, and live `hr_ot_on_file` uses `o.status = 'approved'`. The live finalize body has no `hr_leave_requests` reference. Live `hr_pay_month_guard` raises `hr_month_settled` from the settlement's month on, and it is called by `hr_attendance_guard_finalized` and `hr_overtime_guard_finalized`, which fires on any UPDATE. approveCore writes attendance first (:331) and stops with "NOT approved" on a refusal (:332). `payrollCompute.js:260` docks only absent, unpaid leave and half days. PayrollMonthStatus.jsx:57-58 already counts pending leave and overtime for payroll, so the check exists on the payroll side and is missing on the settlement side.
- Status: NEW. It extends BONUS-LEDGERS-3, which found the same gap for pending travel claims.
- Fix: depends on owner question 1. For option (a): from the leave rows the page already reads, plus a pending-overtime read, list the leaver's pending leave requests starting on or before the last working day and pending overtime in the final month. Block Finalize until each is approved or rejected; approving marks the days, so the settlement recomputes. When a saved draft opens, compare its leave days with the current earned balance and say so. ### GAP-PAY-STATE-3 [P2] A leaver can be paid both the full festival allowance and the settlement's festival share, because each side checks the other only on a page loaded earlier, and nothing in the database checks it

## Dropped in verification
- "A leaver settled while an earlier, already-paid payroll month is reopened is paid that month's travel claims a second time" (reviewer's finding 5): merged into SETTLEMENT-2 at the verifier's correction, which the judge accepted. It has the same root cause and the same fix (`settlement_prior_month_open` refuses while any earlier fiscal-year run is a draft, and a reopened run is one). Carry two things into SETTLEMENT-2 at synthesis. First, widen its premise from "before the previous month's payroll is finalized" to "while any earlier month's run is a draft, including one reopened after salaries were paid". Second, add its larger consequence: live `reopen_payroll_run` puts that run's Payroll-paid claims back to Approved and deletes its advance cuts, while the salary payment stands (S782). A settlement finalized before the re-Finalize then pays those claims again (live `finalize_final_settlement` pays every approved claim, with no date bound) and recovers the cut again. Bhadra's regenerated payslip then shows the leaver as overpaid by the claim (paymentState "over"), money that cannot be recovered once he has left. Optional page addition: Payroll's Reopen confirm names any leaver in the run who holds a draft settlement.

Seen and not raised (not defects as things stand):
- `finalize_final_settlement` reads outstanding advances and recovers them in a second statement without row-locking `hr_advances` (`reopen_payroll_run` does lock). A repayment or write-off committing in that sub-millisecond gap would recover less than net payout assumed. Theoretical.
- A month-end leaver whose last month payroll already paid: `settlement_salary_paid` tells the Owner to Undo a real transfer, and the settlement pays the month again. Decided in S791. Help says nothing is netted off automatically, and the records reconcile if the Owner nets it by hand.
- An incentive earned in a leaver's last month but put in a later pay month is flagged "Left before this run". Choosing the earned month as the pay month pays it.
- Crest Staff requests sent before settlement land pending, and a settled leaver cannot submit, because both submit RPCs assert active access. The pending-at-settlement consequences are GAP-PAY-STATE-2 (leave, overtime) and BONUS-LEDGERS-3 (TADA).

## Owner questions from this area
1. **When someone leaves while a leave request or an overtime entry of theirs is still waiting for a decision, what should Final Settlement do? (GAP-PAY-STATE-2, together with BONUS-LEDGERS-3 for travel claims.)** Example: Bikash asked for 4 days' leave in his last week and a supervisor logged overtime for him, and nobody decided either before his settlement. Today the settlement pays those 4 days as worked and also cashes them out as unused leave, the overtime is never paid, and afterwards neither can be approved. Options:
   - (a) Refuse to finalize until you approve or reject each one, and name them on the settlement screen.
   - (b) Warn and let you finalize, knowing the leave will be paid as worked and the overtime will not be paid.
   - (c) Keep it as today, with no mention.

   Recommended: (a). It takes one click per request, the settlement then pays exactly what was decided, and it matches what we recommend for pending travel claims.
2. **If you reopen a leaver's settlement after that month's payroll has already been run without them (because the settlement was paying that month), what should Crest do? (GAP-PAY-STATE-4.)** Example: Ram's settlement paid Ashwin, Ashwin's payroll left him out, then Ram decided to stay and you set him back to Active. Nobody now pays his Ashwin salary. Options:
   - (a) Warn you at Reopen, naming the payroll month that left him out, and keep a flag on that payroll month until he is paid there or by a finalized settlement.
   - (b) Refuse to reopen the settlement until you reopen that month's payroll first.
   - (c) Keep it as today.

   Recommended: (a). Most reopens are small fixes that end with finalizing again for the same month, which is already correct. The flag catches the rarer case where the leaving is cancelled or moved.
