### ENGINE-1 [P0] Daily-wage staff lose half a day's pay on every approved half-day leave
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollCompute.js:212` (daily `workedDays`), `:190` (`present_days`). The statuses are written for every pay basis by `LeaveManagement.jsx:327-330` and `backfillApprovedLeave.js:107-110`. The wrong formula is also shown in `PayslipCalculation.jsx:142` and taught in `hrGuideData.js:395`.
- What happens: a daily-wage kitchen helper on NPR 1,000 a day asks for a First Half off against Annual Leave (a paid type) in the Staff app. It is approved, the day becomes `half_paid_leave`, and she works the second half. Payroll pays her NPR 500 for that day. It should pay NPR 1,000: the leave half is paid and she worked the other half. On a half day of unpaid leave (`half_unpaid_leave`) she is paid NPR 0 for the half she worked, not NPR 500. A monthly colleague making the same requests is treated as decided: the paid half-day costs nothing and the unpaid one costs half a day. Once the run is finalized, the shortfall is on the payslip, in the cash paid, and in her SSF, because SSF is taken on the smaller earned figure.
- Evidence: `:212` `workedDays = t.present + t.half_day * 0.5 + t.paid_leave + t.half_paid_leave * 0.5 + t.holiday`. It has no `half_unpaid_leave` term and pays only the leave half of a `half_paid_leave` day. Monthly `:260` docks 0 for `half_paid_leave` and 0.5 for `half_unpaid_leave`, and `payrollCompute.test.js:221-235` asserts exactly that. The S309 decision (memory `nepal_payroll_law.md`: "a half-day of a paid type costs the employee nothing … a half-day of an unpaid type deducts exactly 0.5 day") does not depend on pay basis. `hr_attendance` holds one row per employee-day (live unique index), so the worked half cannot be recorded separately. `tallyAttendance` (`:42-55`) adds nothing else. No test covers a daily employee on either status. Hourly is consistent: typed hours plus 4 h for a paid half.
- Status: missed since S309 (5d16c78a). Not caught by the S748-S753 re-analyses. The guide and the Calculation page describe the code, not a decision. hss-suite has the same line (`C:/HSS/src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollCompute.js:190`), and its CROSS-REPO half-day row covers only the CHECK constraint.
- Fix: daily `workedDays = present + half_day*0.5 + paid_leave + half_paid_leave*1 + half_unpaid_leave*0.5 + holiday`. Add `t.half_unpaid_leave * 0.5` to `present_days`. Add daily tests for both statuses. Correct `PayslipCalculation.jsx:142` and `hrGuideData.js:395`, which also leaves out the S749 holiday credit for daily and hourly staff. Open drafts will then show "moved" on gross, which is correct. File the fix in hss-suite's `docs/CROSS-REPO.md`. Finalized months that already underpaid need a one-off list (finalized payslips of daily staff with either status) and a manual top-up: see the owner question.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading. The arithmetic follows directly from the line.
- Verified: verifier confirmed at P1. Judge confirmed and raised it to P0. It meets the P0 test (wages paid wrong and persisted on a finalized payslip). It is the normal path for a daily worker's half-day leave, not an edge case. Nothing on screen reveals it, because the Calculation page prints the wrong formula as the rule.

### ENGINE-2 [P1] Women on the payroll may be over-withheld income tax, because the 10% women's rebate is never applied
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/tds.js:104-114` (`applySlabs`) and its callers `:143` (`computeMonthlyTdsBreakdown`), `:192` (`computeFinalMonthTds`) and `:203-209` (`computeBonusTds`); `bonusTax.js:122-142`. `hr_employees.gender` exists (`EmployeeForm.jsx:359-363`: male / female / other), but no engine reads it, and it is not in `PAYROLL_EMPLOYEE_COLUMNS` (`payrollData.js:351`).
- What happens: Nepal's schedule gives a resident woman whose only income is employment a 10% rebate on the computed tax. Crest never applies it, so every payslip, festival or incentive run and final settlement for such a woman withholds and deposits up to 10% too much tax, and the TDS certificate prints the larger figure. Under FY 2083/84 the harm is small for most F&B staff. A non-SSF manager on NPR 70,000 a month (8.4 lakh) owes 1% = NPR 8,400, so she is over-withheld about NPR 840 a year. An SSF contributor under 10 lakh owes nothing either way. It grows above 10 lakh: at 15 lakh non-SSF the tax is NPR 60,000 and the rebate NPR 6,000.
- Evidence: a grep of `src/modules/hr` for rebate or female finds only the gender picker. `nepal_payroll_law.md` and `hr-payroll.md` record no rebate and no decision against one. Law: notarynepal.com and economicfrontline.com (FY 2083/84 guides) describe the rebate as current. Both say to confirm it against the gazetted Finance Act 2083, which the 2083/84 budget speech did not explicitly restate.
- Status: never applied by the live engine. S405 (a3cd2396) deleted the unused `src/shared/constants/taxSlabs.js`, which carried `FEMALE_TAX_REBATE = 0.10`. The commit called the file "legally-wrong", while its own CHANGELOG line (`CHANGELOG/S400-S449.md:522`) says "flagged, pending user decision". It was an engineering judgement with no law check and no owner decision on the rebate. hss-suite keeps the same rebate as an open accountant question (`C:/HSS/docs/BUILD-LOG.md:10031`, `:10908`).
- Fix: after the accountant confirms, add one helper `annualTaxFor({ taxable, slabs, isSsf, isFemale })` = `applySlabs(...) × (isFemale ? 0.9 : 1)`. Use it in `computeMonthlyTdsBreakdown`, `computeFinalMonthTds` and both sides of `computeBonusTds`. Pass `emp.gender === 'female'`: add `gender` to `PAYROLL_EMPLOYEE_COLUMNS`, the festival and incentive employee columns and the settlement employee read. Show the rebate line in the TDS breakdown panel and the payslip working.
- Confidence: plausible. The rebate is sourced for FY 2082/83. Whether it survives in the Finance Act 2083 is not verified.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections. The status was changed from NEW to a challenge of the S405 deletion. The example was moved from 2082/83 figures (NPR 4,500) to 2083/84 figures. Judge confirmed at P1, pending the law.

### ENGINE-3 [P2] Final Settlement pre-fills too few leave days to pay out when the leaver has approved leave booked after their last day, or leave from an earlier spell this year
- Where: `src/modules/hr/leave/leaveBalance.js:28-35` (`leaveUsed`, no date bound) and `:38-47` (`leaveEncashed`, no spell bound); `src/modules/hr/settlement/FinalSettlement.jsx:253` (the leave read selects `start_date` only), `:309-312` and `:327` (the pre-fill); `settlementCompute.js:43-51`.
- What happens: a waiter on NPR 20,000 basic with an 18-day annual quota has 5 days of Dashain leave approved in Shrawan for Ashwin. He resigns with his last day on 15 Bhadra. Earned leave is 18 × 4/12 = 6 days. The 5 Dashain days he will never take are subtracted, so the box pre-fills 1 day instead of 6, about NPR 3,846 short. Settlement Finalize does not cancel future leave. The hint labels the 5 days "taken". Typing more than the remaining figure raises a warning (`:618-620`) telling the operator to keep extra days "only for carry-forward you are sure of", which discourages the correction. A rehire in the same BS year is hit the same way: earned leave restarts at the new join date, but the earlier spell's taken and paid-out days are still subtracted.
- Evidence: `leaveUsed` filters employee, type, `approved` and `bsYearOf(start_date)` only. `leaveEncashed` filters on `bsYearOf(last_working_date)` only. `:253` reads no `end_date` or `day_type`, so the request cannot be bounded. `:327` `setLeaveDays(... String(leaveBal.remaining) ...)`. Live: neither `finalize_final_settlement` nor `reopen_final_settlement` touches `hr_leave_requests`.
- Status: missed by S752.
- Fix: give `leaveUsed` an optional `{ from, until }` window that counts only chargeable days on or after the current join date and on or before the last working date, and prorates a request that straddles the last day. The settlement passes both dates and selects `end_date`. Give `leaveEncashed` the same `from` (only settlements with `last_working_date >= join_date`). The Balances tab keeps calling it without a window. Optionally list the leaver's future approved leave on the settlement page with a Cancel action.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading.
- Verified: verifier confirmed; judge confirmed. The box is editable and the defect needs future-dated leave or a same-year rehire, so P2.

### ENGINE-4 [P2] Festival Allowance pays some leavers one month's share too few
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/bonusTax.js:152-153` (`until = endRaw && endRaw < ref ? endRaw : ref`) and `:169`; called from `FestivalAllowance.jsx:312` with the 15th of the pay month (`:69`). Contrast `settlementCompute.js:79,130`, whose own festival share uses `dayAfter(last day)`.
- What happens: this needs a last working day between the 1st and the 14th of the pay month, with the next day a join-day anniversary. A cook on NPR 24,000 basic joined on 11 Magh 2082 and his last working day is 10 Ashwin 2083. He is in the Ashwin Dashain run because his end date is in the pay month and no finalized settlement has paid his festival share yet. He worked 8 complete months, but the loop stops at the 11 Ashwin anniversary because it is after 10 Ashwin. He gets 24,000 × 7/12 = NPR 14,000 instead of NPR 16,000. A colleague who joined the same day and is still employed gets 8 months. `completedServiceMonths` treats its end as exclusive, which is right for the festival date and one day short for an inclusive last working day.
- Evidence: `:153` uses `end_date` as is, and `:169` breaks on `anniversary > until`. `payrollS751.test.js:214-216` tests a leaver only with an end date that is not the day before an anniversary. `settlementCompute.js:79` uses `serviceUntil = dayAfter(lastAd)`, so the two paths disagree.
- Status: missed by S751.
- Fix: `const endExcl = endRaw ? dayAfter(endRaw) : null; const until = endExcl && endExcl < ref ? endExcl : ref` (`dayAfter` from `gratuityCompute.js:29`). Add a test with the end date one day before an anniversary. The existing test at `payrollS751.test.js:216` still returns 3.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections. The reviewer's example (last day 20 Ashwin) does not reproduce, because `until` is capped at 15 Ashwin. The scenario above is the corrected one. Judge confirmed.

### ENGINE-5 [P2] A leaver rehired later in the same fiscal year is under-withheld TDS, because year-to-date income ignores their Final Settlement
- Where: `src/modules/hr/payroll/payrollData.js:19-44` (`fetchYtdMap` reads finalized payslips and bonuses only) and `:49-79` (`ytdFromPayslips`); `bonusTax.js:51-65` (`payslipYtdForFy`, the same gap for festival and incentive tax).
- What happens: a non-SSF head cook on NPR 1,10,000 a month leaves on 20 Kartik. His Final Settlement pays Kartik's salary, leave encashment and festival share, and withholds tax on them. He is rehired on 1 Falgun, which S791 made payable. From Falgun, year-to-date income leaves out Kartik's pay, the lump sums and the tax withheld on them, and counts one month too few. The rest of the year is projected too low, so once the year crosses a band (10 lakh in 2083/84) monthly TDS under-withholds. The TDS certificate does read settlements, so it shows a year taxed below its slab, and the employer carries the shortfall. Under 2083/84 this matters only near or above 10 lakh a year.
- Evidence: `payrollData.js:30` selects from `hr_payslips` only, and `:33` `fetchFinalizedBonuses` covers festival and incentive rows only. Live information_schema: `hr_final_settlements` stores `month_gross`, `month_absence_deduction`, `month_ot_amount`, `month_ssf_employee`, `month_retirement_contribution`, `month_tds`, `lump_tds`, `leave_encashment`, `gratuity`, `festival_pro` and `notice_pay`. hr-payroll.md S752 says filing sheets must read settlements, and `fetchYtdMap` is the monthly-TDS equivalent that was left out.
- Status: missed by S791, which made a same-FY rehire payable. It is the same defect as PAYROLL review finding 3, which adds the consumers `PayrollRun.jsx:247` and `FinalSettlement.jsx:257`. Merge the two into one entry in HR_TODO.
- Fix: in `fetchYtdMap`, also read finalized `hr_final_settlements` (paged, `.order('id')`). Fold in each one whose settle month is earlier in the current FY: gross += month earned + lump sums, ssf += `month_ssf_employee`, retirement += `month_retirement_contribution`, withheld += `month_tds + lump_tds`, count += 1. Add the lump amounts to `e.bonus` and `lump_tds` to `e.bonusWithheld`, so the settled-at-source split in tds.js stays consistent. Apply the same fold to `payslipYtdForFy`. Keep the error-not-empty contract. No migration: the columns exist.
- Confidence: confirmed by reading. The money effect needs a same-FY rehire whose year crosses a band.
- Verified: verifier confirmed with corrections (marked as a duplicate of PAYROLL-3, fix detail added above). Judge confirmed.

## Dropped in verification
None. All five findings survived. For the record, the reviewer saw the following and chose not to raise them. They are not defects as things stand:
- Hourly: an approved OT entry with more hours than the sheet's OT can pay on top of `hours_worked` (2.5×) when a manager types the OT hours into hours worked. Whether this happens depends on how the day is entered.
- Adding the employer's 20% SSF to assessable income before the one-third retirement cap. It differs only when CIT pushes the bucket past the cap. This is a question for the accountant.
- A bonus paid in Ashadh, or in a leaver's final month, is never trued up by monthly TDS. The error is only projection drift.
- Daily and hourly SSF contributors get no projected SSF relief in the bonus base. This is negligible under the 2083/84 slabs.
- `daysInBsMonth` falls back to 30 outside the 2000-2087 table.
- Monthly OT is priced on basic / (days in the BS month × 8), so a 32-day month pays about 10% less per OT hour than a 29-day one. It follows the never-assume-30 rule. The law's divisor is unverified and is a question for the accountant.

## Owner questions from this area
1. **The 10% income-tax discount for women (ENGINE-2).** Nepal's tax rules give a woman whose only income is her salary a 10% discount on her income tax. Crest does not apply it, so women on your payroll may be having a little too much tax taken: about NPR 840 a year for a manager on NPR 70,000 a month, more for higher earners. Options: (a) apply it automatically to every employee marked Female; (b) apply it only when you tick "salary is her only income" on the employee; (c) leave it off. Recommended: (a), once your accountant confirms the discount still applies in FY 2083/84. An employer cannot see an employee's other income, and payroll in Nepal usually applies it by default.
2. **Daily-wage staff underpaid on half-day leave in months already paid (ENGINE-1).** Once the formula is fixed, new months are paid correctly, but finalized months stay as they were. Options: (a) Crest lists every daily-wage employee who had an approved half-day leave in a finalized month, with the amount owed, and you pay it as a one-off top-up (recommended, because it is wages owed); (b) fix only from now on.
