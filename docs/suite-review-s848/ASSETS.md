The Act: 7% × 4,00,000 (the base the 15% is applied to) = 28,000 deductible, 12,000 carried. 11,000 of deduction moves into later years at 15% a year: about NPR 2,750 more tax this year at a 25% rate, on a schedule that Post then locks.
