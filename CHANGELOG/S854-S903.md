### S856 — 2026-10-21 — PERF-5: Stock Report, Reorder Report and the Dashboard walk the recipe book in memory; the Food Cost trend starts at once

The last open engineering item in `IMS_TODO.md` (owner: "fix ims"). Fewer round trips only; every figure is

- **Stock Report and Reorder Report take the recipe book (S793's pattern, as Variance and Stock Movements).**
  `useRecipeBook()` starts in `init()` beside the shared month list, the book rides in the period batch

  `explodeRecipeIngredients(…, { book })`. Before, the walk started after the month's reads landed and fetched

  closing, wastage, staff meals) and the month's `sales_entries` on the Dashboard use `fetchAllRowsParallel`
