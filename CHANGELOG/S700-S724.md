### S700 — 2026-09-08 — The dashboards get one vertical rhythm, and the KPI card stops being four cards

**Service worker `crest-v236` → `crest-v238`. No migration, no Edge Function deploy.**

Started from a screenshot: the weekday initials under Daily Purchases vs Sales were cut off. They
were not cramped — Recharts places tick text at `axisY + tickSize(6) + tickMargin(2)` even with
`tickLine={false}`, so in a 160px card with a 26px axis band the second line's baseline landed at
**162px inside a 160px SVG** and the SVG's own edge clipped it. Band to 34, card to 184.

That prompted a spacing analysis of all five dashboards, which found **14 distinct vertical
intervals** against a documented 4/8/16/24 scale — and four real defects.

**Group Console and Owner Dashboard butted shadowed cards edge to edge.** `.stat-grid` is `gap: 0`
because `.stat-card` collapses each pair of adjacent borders into ONE drawn line, via `-1px`
margins, with its shadow dropped to do it. GroupDashboard put `.card` in that grid and
OwnerDashboard put a hand-rolled box in it — neither carries the seam pull or the shadow reset, so
thirteen KPI cells rendered 2px double rules with their shadows overlapping at every join. Exactly
the "two depth models arguing" the class comment warns about, in the two files that had not read it.

**"Show 6 more" sat flush against the chart row.** The disclosure toggle had `marginTop: 10` and
nothing below it, and `imsChartsAndTables` opens with a row carrying `marginBottom` and no
`marginTop` — so the gap under a control was **0 collapsed and 14 expanded**. The space under a
control must not depend on what the control says.

**The three module columns did not start on the same line.** HR carried `marginTop: showIms ? 6 : 0`
and POS the same — written when the module blocks STACKED. Once S438/S439 made them grid columns
that 6px stopped being a separator and became a column OFFSET, so Inventory's heading sat 6px above
Human Resources' and Point of Sale's. On the phone the same margins compounded the other way:
14 + the grid's own 16px row-gap + 6 = **36px** between modules against 14 everywhere else.

**Sections did not read as sections.** ClientDashboard put 10px between a module heading and its
own content and 14px between one section and the next — a **1.4x** contrast. HrDashboard states the
right target in a comment and hits it: 8px label→content, 28px group→group, 3.5x. Below that ratio
the heading floats free of the block it names. `.dash-section` (28) and `.dash-row` (16) are the
pair now, both self-zeroing on `:last-child` — which is what makes them safe to apply
unconditionally, since dashboard JSX is conditional almost everywhere and which block renders last
depends on which modules a client bought. They step to 16/8 under 768px, where `.main-content`
drops 32 → 16 and a 28px group gap would separate sections by nearly twice the page's own margin.

**The Admin Dashboard shifted layout on load**: its skeleton grid was `minmax(190px)` / `gap: 14` /
`alignItems: start` against the real strip's `158` / `8` / `stretch`. A skeleton that does not match
its own content is a layout shift with extra steps.

**And the KPI card was four cards.** ClientDashboard, OwnerDashboard and AdminDashboardOverview had
each typed `.stat-card`'s five box properties out inline and then landed on their own padding —
`10px 14px`, `14px 16px`, `12px 14px` against the class's `20px`. `stat-card--compact` is the dense
tier as a real class (`8px 16px`, no seam pull, shadow kept, for a strip that has a real gap), and
`card--compact` (16px) replaces the six inline `.card` padding overrides, ChartCard's included. The
declared 8px gutters in `.stat-grid--compact` had also been rendering as **7px**, because
`.stat-card`'s seam pull is meaningless once there is space between cells.

Everything else was normalisation: 14 distinct vertical values down to 4/6/8/16/28 across the five
files, with `.btn`'s own `8px 16px` and 6px icon gap taken instead of five hand-rolled button
paddings. Two values were deliberately left alone — the adoption pills' measured `gap: 3` (S642
sized that row against the grid floor; changing it re-opens a wrap that was measured) and the
`4px 6px` / `-4px -6px` hit-area pair, whose halves must match.

The cascade was verified by measurement rather than by reading: `.stat-grid dash-section` resolves
to 28 at 1440 and 16 at 390, `.dash-row` inside a `.stat-grid--compact` resolves to 16 (the later
declaration wins the tie, which is why `.dash-section`/`.dash-row` are declared after both grids),
and both zero on `:last-child` at each width. The hover transition the three `kpiCard()`s were each
declaring inline moved onto `.interactive-card`, where the border it animates lives.
