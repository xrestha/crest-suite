### S776 — 2026-09-17 — POS critique fix pass: a bill close cannot hang or be blamed on another till, and a till lock keeps the unsent order

**Service worker bump (`crest-v328`). No migration, no Edge Function deploy.** Stage 1 of 6 of the fix
pass on the POS critique (`.impeccable/critique/2026-09-17T07-02-03Z__src-modules-pos.md`, 24/40, P1 #1).
Files: `src/modules/pos/orders/PosOrders.jsx`, `src/utils/withTimeout.js` (+test),
`src/shared/errorText.js` (+test), `src/pages/Help.js`, `src/pages/settings/posGuideData.js`,
`.claude/rules/pos-billing.md`, `public/service-worker.js`, `src/shared/appVersion.js`.

**What was wrong.** The payment close had no time limit anywhere, and Cancel is disabled while a close
runs, so a stalled request left the cashier on "Processing…" with a guest waiting and no way out. If
the close write landed but its reply was lost, a second press re-saved the order, the server refused
it as already closed, and the till said the bill was "billed or voided on another till". It wasn't,
and it never printed. The bill also printed only after Inventory posting and the loyalty award, and
the close ended on the floor with nothing said.

**What changed.**
- Every wait on the close path is bounded (`bounded()`, 20 s a step; the save and the Inventory post
  30 s). The Confirm button names the step it is on.
- The close write is marked before it goes out (`closeAttemptRef`). A timeout or dropped connection
  reads the bill back (`settleUnknownClose`): closed by this login finishes it; still open says
  press again; unreadable says it is not known and not to take the payment again. The next press and
  Cancel both settle it first, so a landed close is finished and printed, never blamed on another till,
  never charged twice.
- New order after the close (owner decision): Split legs, then the print, then table, booking,
  Inventory, customer and loyalty (`finishClosedBill`). Print counters are no longer awaited, and a
  Complimentary Slip whose food cost cannot be read in time is not printed half-valued.
- The floor ends on a closing line, e.g. "PB24 closed · Takeaway #31 · NPR 200 · change NPR 50"
  (`role="status"`; the floor banner had no live region).
- `withTimeout` marks its rejection (`isTimeout`), and `errorText` words a timeout without claiming
  the request did or did not land. A loyalty award that timed out says "not known", not "add them by
  hand".

**Verified.** ESLint clean; `withTimeout`, `errorText` and the POS orders tests pass (61). Live on the
BHATTI CHOILA dummy client from the dev server, with Playwright:
1. The close PATCH reached the server and its response was aborted. The till read the bill back,
   printed it and showed "PB24 closed · Takeaway #31 · NPR 200 · change NPR 50"; the row: billed,
   invoice 24, `closed_by` this login, `print_count` 1.
2. The same, with the read-back aborted too. The modal said it is not known whether Takeaway #32
   closed; the next press with the network back finished it ("PB25 closed · Takeaway #32 · NPR 200"),
   one invoice (25), `print_count` 1.
3. An ordinary void: "Takeaway #33 voided — no bill was issued.", `ims_posted_at` stamped, kitchen
   ticket cancelled.
Test tickets for #31 and #32 were marked served so the dummy client's KDS does not alert.

### S775 — 2026-09-17 — Attendance imports a month of punches from the fingerprint machine's Excel or CSV

**Service worker bump (`crest-v327`). No migration, no Edge Function deploy.** Files:
`src/modules/hr/attendance/attendanceImport.js` (new), `attendanceImportPlan.js` (new),
`AttendanceImportModal.jsx` (new), `attendanceImport.test.js` (new),
`AttendanceImportModal.test.jsx` (new), `AttendanceSheet.jsx`, `src/pages/Help.js`,
`src/pages/settings/hrGuideData.js`, `.claude/rules/hr-payroll.md`, `public/service-worker.js`,
`src/shared/appVersion.js`.

**What it does.** "↑ Import from machine" beside the month picker, in three steps: choose the file,
confirm who is who, check what will change. The days land on the sheet as unsaved marks, and the
sheet's own Save writes them.

**It reads many layouts, because every machine exports differently.** It recognises a month grid
(Casa Açaí Cafe's ZKTeco "Monthly Check In&Out Report", "08:05-20:00" per day), one row per person
per day (Date / In / Out), and a raw punch list with or without a heading row. It finds the heading
row under any title block, reads BS or AD dates in either order, Excel time cells, 12-hour clocks,
and a hand-kept register's P / A / Off / Leave marks. If it doesn't recognise the layout, the reader
picks the columns. A PDF is refused, with a request for the Excel export.

**Four decisions taken with Aashish (2026-09-17):**

1. **People are confirmed on every import.** Nothing is remembered and no column was added. Crest
   guesses from the names ("ananad" → Anand) and from an employee code equal to the machine ID.
2. **A day with no punch follows the roster.** Rostered to work → Absent. Rostered off → Off, or
   the leave or holiday the shift name says. Not rostered → left blank.
3. **An incomplete day is flagged.** One punch, or in and out under an hour apart, comes in as
   Present with hours blank. It stays amber until fixed, and Save asks once if any are unsaved.
4. **Blanks are filled and Present days take the machine's times.** Each change is listed and can be
   unticked. Leave, Off, Holiday, Absent and Half-day are never touched. A punch on one of those
   days is listed as a difference.

The import also never marks a day after today, a day with no punch yet today, or a day before
someone joined or after they left. It refuses when the month is locked or the roster could not be
read.

**Found while building it:** a date reading ranked by "most dates inside the month" put one column of
a Bhadra grid into Shrawan ("05-04" read day-first is Shrawan 5). It now ranks by real dates first.
Separately, SheetJS turns a CSV's BS "05-01" into 30 April 2001 unless the file is read as text.

**Verified:** 64 attendance tests pass, including the real Bhadra rows (4 people, 31 days, the three
one-punch days flagged) and a dialog test that runs a generated .xlsx through file → match → review →
apply. The real .xlsx was also read end to end through the same SheetJS call the dialog makes. Not yet
clicked through in the running app.
