### S790 — 2026-09-24 — A setup guide for new clients: a checklist that ticks when the work is really done

Reported from a screenshot of the Admin Dashboard: "some major steps are missing in guiding the
client… for basic computer users in Nepal", with the idea of a checkbox per feature that ticks when
it is visited and a card that goes away when all are ticked. Researched first (onboarding-checklist
practice, first-time computer users in Nepal and South Asia, how restaurant software sets a new
outlet up), then the owner took twelve decisions in plain words.

- **The research, in short.** Tick when the work is DONE, not when a page is opened: looking feels
  like learning without being learning (Deslauriers et al., PNAS 2019), and checklist vendors
  say the same. Short lists get finished; long ones and auto-started tours do not. A person helping
  in the first week is the most reliable help for novice users (Medhi et al., ACM TOCHI 2011).
  In Nepal most adults use a phone rather than a computer; formal Nepali interfaces have gone
  unused while everyday Nepali with familiar English words helps (Hall, Ghimire & Newton, 2009);
  Viber and Messenger reach people more than WhatsApp.
- **The owner's decisions.** A box ticks by itself when the saved thing exists; opening a page
  shows "Started, not saved yet". Every step can be skipped and the card hidden to one line,
  remembered on the person's login. A client with several modules is first asked what to set up
  first. The Owner and email-login managers see it (no staff rank, no PIN logins). It appears by
  itself only for new clients (an approved trial or the first 60 days after approval), and any
  established client can start it: "Start the setup guide" in Help → Getting Started, reached from
  the account menu's new "Setup guide" item (asked for after seeing it running). Every new client is offered a setup call (Call, WhatsApp, Viber) and Crest enters their item
  list for them. A "Check what your bill will say" step before the first real customer. English
  only and no videos for now. A visible Help button, and a "Setup step N of M" strip on the page a
  step opens. The first bill is a real customer (no practice mode); the printer is not a step.
- **What the client sees.** `SetupGuideCard` replaces `GettingStartedCard` on the dashboard and sits
  at the top of Help → Getting Started. Start here (account ready, setup call), then one part per
  module: Stock & costing (items with the unit rule, suppliers, opening count, first supplier bill;
  then menu and sales for a non-POS client, dish costing on Growth), Till & bills (menu prices,
  bill details, tables, till tablet, first real customer; then cashier PINs, closing the day, the
  sales report), Staff & payroll (staff, pay, this year's holidays, one day's attendance; then the
  Crest Staff app), and the first month-end in its last five days (closing count, close the month,
  first payroll). Each step says what to press, where to find it later, and a caution where a step
  cannot be undone. When everything is done it says so once, with a habit per module, and goes.

  HR-only clients never got one; "Bill your first order" opened Till Devices (manager-only) and

### S788 — 2026-09-24 — Payroll: a payment to someone regenerated out of the month is no longer dropped

Filed by hss-suite in S787 while porting S782. A salary payment is keyed by run + employee and
survives Reopen by design. If a month was reopened after someone was paid and Regenerate then left
them out (their end date moved, for example), they had no payslip, and `runPaymentSummary()` walked
payslips only: their money vanished from the paid total, nothing read as overpaid, and the Undo for
that payment was on no row of the page.

- **`runPaymentSummary()` walks payslips and payments.** Anyone with a payment but no payslip is
  treated as a payslip of 0: counted in `over` and `paidTotal`, not in `owed` or `paid` (there is no
  salary of theirs left to settle), and listed in a new `noPayslip` while an active payment stands
  against them. Two tests, including the all-undone case.
- **Payroll Run gives them their own row**: name, "No payslip in Bhadra any more — paid, then left
  out when the month was regenerated", and the Paid cell with its overpaid wording and the ⋯ Undo.
  The page now also fetches names for employees who appear only in the payments, so the row never
  reads "(employee record not found)".
- **The month strip checks "overpaid" before "Nothing to pay".** It says "N paid more than their
  payslip — check Payroll" whenever `over > 0`; but a run whose remaining payslips all net 0 has
  `owed` 0, and the old order showed a green "✓ Nothing to pay" over the money. Two tests.
- **Caught in review, before commit:** the first draft kept any `over` row out of `owed`/`paid`
  when its net was 0 — which also dropped a REAL payslip that nets 0 but was paid, a regression from
  S782 (the strip read "Nothing to pay" over it). Now only the no-payslip case is kept out. Both
  fixes were watched to fail with the bug planted. hss-suite's copy had the same line; fixed there too.
- `CACHE_NAME` / `APP_VERSION` → `crest-v347`. `hr-payroll.md` gained the rule. Both
  `docs/CROSS-REPO.md` ledgers mark the item fixed.

### S779 — 2026-09-18 — An unsaved purchase bill survives the page closing, and the VAT tick moves next to Amount

Reported from the till side: leave the browser for another app and the bill being keyed was gone,
start again. The reporter added that Chrome had auto-updated itself — which is the whole story.

**What was happening.** `PurchaseBillForm` held an entire vendor bill — vendor, day, discount, the
supplier's printed figures and a row per item — in React state and nowhere else until Save. A bill
keyed off paper is commonly 10–20 lines, so anything that ends the page's life took twenty minutes
of typing with it, silently and with no warning: Chrome restarting itself for an auto-update, a
phone or tablet discarding a backgrounded tab to reclaim memory, Chrome's own memory saver on a
laptop, and this app's `recoverFromChunkError()` reload when a deploy lands mid-entry. None of
those are failures the form can prevent, and the app had no draft state of any kind.

**The fix.** New `src/modules/ims/purchases/purchaseBillDraft.js` mirrors the bill into
`localStorage` as it is typed and reads it back on the next mount:

- **Two write triggers, because they cover different deaths.** A 400ms debounce catches the ordinary
  case at typing speed without writing per keystroke; `visibilitychange` (hidden) and `pagehide`
  write *now*, because hidden is the last event a page is guaranteed to see before the OS discards
  it or the browser restarts, and the debounce would not have fired for the last fraction of a
  second of typing.
- **`localStorage`, not `sessionStorage`.** A browser that restarts itself opens a new session — the
  reported case. Deliberately not the offline IndexedDB queue either: that queue *replays* writes,
  and a half-typed bill must never be written by anything but the person who finishes it.
- **A draft is only ever the DIFFERENCE from the bill as opened** (`billDraftSignature` over the
  header and lines, minus the per-mount `_key`). On a new bill that means "something has been
  typed"; on an edit, "there are unsaved corrections". Opening a bill and touching nothing, or
  undoing back to where you started, leaves nothing to restore.
- **Keyed by the bill AND by the login** — `edit:<purchase_group_id>:<profile>` or
  `new:<period_id>:<profile>`. The bill half is a UUID, so drafts are already unique across clients
  on a device an admin switches tenants on and no client id is needed or stored. The login half is
  the S731 rule ("a cache outlives the session that filled it") applied to a store that outlives one
  deliberately: Crest runs on shared store-room tablets where a PIN session ends on an idle lock, so

**What it does.** "↑ Import from machine" beside the month picker, in three steps: choose the file,

**Four decisions taken with Aashish (2026-09-17):**

   unticked. Leave, Off, Holiday, Absent and Half-day are never touched. A punch on one of those
   days is listed as a difference.

**What an owner will notice.**
- **A printed bill cannot be changed or deleted by anyone**, Owner and operator included — a
  mistake is corrected with a Credit Note. Before this, a closed bill's lines, payments, buyer PAN
  and amount could all be edited or deleted over the REST API from a till login, a Staff-rank waiter's PIN included.
- **Only a Supervisor or above can close a bill**, and the database now records who closed it and
  when from the server, not from the tablet's clock.
- **A bill line's price comes from the menu.** A tablet cannot send its own price. A dish already
  on an order keeps the price it was ordered at if the menu changes mid-meal.
- **Menu prices, VAT and "On POS" are a manager's.** Only a POS manager, an IMS manager or the
  Owner can change them. A dish a Supervisor creates in Recipe Costing arrives OFF the till until a
  manager puts it on from Menu Pricing, where its price is on screen.
- **Credit Notes are manager-only**, and issuing one now asks how the money went back. Cash is
  recorded as a refund out of the open shift's drawer. The note also takes back the loyalty points
  the bill earned and returns any points spent on it.
- **Every tablet gets its own key.** POS Setup lists the activated tablets, when each was last
  used, and a Revoke button for a lost or sold one. Tablets activated before this keep working on
  the old shared key until a manager switches it off in POS Setup.
- **A POS manager has limits on POS Staff.** They cannot change their own login or another
  manager's, cannot make anyone a manager (only the Owner can), and cannot give a discount limit or
  Void permission bigger than their own.

**Owner decisions (Aashish, 2026-09-14), each with its reason:**
- **Update Order asks whether to send the new items to the kitchen now.** Pressing Update and
  forgetting KOT/BOT was how food got added to a bill and never cooked.
- **Taking payment needs an open shift** (Charge and Complimentary; a Void does not), so every bill
  lands on a drawer count. **This changes BHATTI CHOILA's routine:** all 18 of their recent bills
  were charged with no shift open.
- **Closing a shift over open tables warns and lets it close.** Those bills land on whichever shift
  is open when they are charged. This replaces the old "closing is blocked" rule.
- **Tapping Served clears Ready** on the floor and takes the ticket off the Kitchen Display. There
  is a new `served` ticket stage.
- **Sales Exceptions values everything before VAT.** VAT on a sale that never happened was never
  revenue, and counting it made a void look about 13% bigger than a discount on the same food.
- **Sales Report spreads a split bill across the methods it was paid with**, so Payment Summary's
  Cash ties to the shift Z-report.
- **A return is a minus row on the day the Credit Note was issued**, on every tab. The original
  bill stays at full value on its own day. This replaces the S580 rule that left a credit-noted bill
  out of Daily and counted it only as returned quantity.
- **Covers Report counts dine-in only.** Takeaway and delivery get their own line. Credit-noted
  bills are kept, because a credit note corrects the bill, not the fact that guests sat down.
- **The 1L+ tab merges a name-only bill into the same-name PAN party.** The walk-in row is no
  longer flagged "Missing PAN".
- **Parking slips roll over at 6 AM Nepal time**, so a car parked during a late service is still
  on the Open tab after midnight.
- **The Kitchen Display strikes through a pulled or reduced line** on the ticket that sent it,
  with the word "cancelled", so the kitchen stops cooking it.
- **Recent Bills (reprints and the Credit Note entry) is Supervisor+.**
- **Menu Pricing opens for a POS manager, an IMS manager or the Owner**, by URL as well as by nav,
  on any client.
- **An offline order that conflicts offers "Start new order with these"**, so the items come back
  as unsent lines on a new order instead of being lost.
- **Two bookings may not hold the same table at overlapping times.** This is checked on screen
  only: two saves in the same second can still both land (see Still open).
- **The public booking page counts accepted bookings only** towards "Full". An unanswered request
  no longer blocks a slot.
- **A guest QR order with a dish that went off the menu is refused whole**, naming the dish.
- **Priorities for the next builds, in the owner's order:** table move / merge / split, add-ons
  and sizes, then kitchen and bar printers. Service charge was not chosen.
