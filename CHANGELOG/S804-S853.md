Stage 1 of the 2026-10-06 HR `/impeccable critique` (27/40, 2 P1), the owner's "month context first". The
HR Dashboard's strip knew which month was being paid (Bhadra) and every page it linked to forgot it: Payroll

- **Payroll** opens on `?period=` when a link names one, else the strip's own month (`pickStatusPeriod`: last

  still drafts"), with a button that opens each. The page's own Generate, Finalize and Reopen keep the note
  current.

  a month that has ended keeps the primary Generate Payroll.

  TDS are due by the 25th, while the strip moves on to the running month at Finalize (H32). Owner-approved
