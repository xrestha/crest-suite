### S811 — 2026-10-10 — A PAN bill no longer asks whether its line totals include VAT

**Why.** Aashish, from a screenshot of a mango line on a supplier's PAN bill (no VAT charged): the
"Line totals on this bill are After VAT / Before VAT" row, and "after VAT" under the Total heading,
showed on a bill with no VAT box ticked. That read as if the 525 typed off the paper were being
treated as VAT-inclusive. It never was: the choice only moves a ticked line's Rate
(`rateFromLineTotal`), so an unticked line's Rate is Total ÷ Qty either way, and Item Master gets that
price per base unit.

**What changed (`PurchaseBillForm.jsx`).**
- While no line has VAT ticked, the row reads **"No VAT on this bill — each line's Total is used
  exactly as printed, as on a PAN bill. Tick VAT on any line the supplier charged VAT on."** The After /
  Before VAT radios come back as soon as one line is ticked, set as they were left.
- The note takes the row's place rather than removing it, so ticking the first VAT box does not push
  the table down under the pointer.
- The Total heading's second line says **"no VAT"** in that state, and its tooltip says the choice
  appears once a line has VAT ticked.
- Nothing stored changes: `totals_basis` was never saved, and `purchase_entries` holds the same rate.

**Help and guide.** Help → Purchases and the IMS module guide (Line totals entry) say what a PAN bill
does.

**Verified.** `billLineVat`, `purchaseBillDraft`, `purchaseLines` and `appVersion` tests: 48 of 48 pass.
`build:verify` clean. Not checked in a browser (Playwright MCP failed to connect this session).
