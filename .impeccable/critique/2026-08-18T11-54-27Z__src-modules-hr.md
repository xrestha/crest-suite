## Design Specificity Verdict

## Overall Impression

The HR module is the most domain-literate code in this product and the least design-system-current. Its payroll math shows its work in a way an accountant can audit line by line, and its tooltips do the plain-language job the standing rule asks for better than anywhere else in the app. What it hasn't had is the maintenance the rest of the codebase received over the last several sessions — every app-wide sweep since S546 missed it, and one of them missed it for a reason worth knowing.

The single biggest opportunity: **make Payroll Run refuse to finalize numbers it knows are stale.** Everything needed already exists — the live-vs-stored comparison runs on `/hr/calculation` using shared helpers from `payrollData.js`. It just isn't wired to the one irreversible button in the module.

## What's Working

1. **The math shows its work.** `PayrollCalculation.jsx`'s `CalcDetail` (~41–183) renders every payslip as a visible running calculation with `× ÷ + =` operator rows, and its ⚠ Stale / ⚠ Missing / ⚠ OT ×2? badges reconcile live math against the stored run. `FinalSettlement.jsx` and `Overtime.jsx` print the formula beside every figure. This is the accountant persona served properly.
2. **Honesty about legal uncertainty.** `GratuityTracker.jsx:128` and `FinalSettlement.jsx:286` state plainly that the 12-month gratuity vesting cliff is "a commonly applied assumption, not confirmed in the Act — verify with an accountant." That is the plain-language rule applied at the highest-stakes point, and it is rare.
3. **Payslip trust engineering.** `PayslipBody.jsx` is one renderer shared by the owner's modal, the print view and the employee's Self-Service copy, so the three cannot drift; the absence row prints a day count because "paid for 22 of 30 days" is auditable against memory where a bare rupee figure is not.
4. **The shell conventions that were adopted are adopted correctly.** Every `data-table` is inside a `table-wrap`; both Fab pages pair `table-wrap--fab-clear`; every tab row uses `tab-bar`/`tab-btn`; no nonexistent badge classes; no inline `.btn:disabled` duplication. Where HR uses the system, it uses it right.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Payroll Run can finalize a stale draft, and only a different page knows it's stale**
- **What:** `PayrollRun.jsx` renders figures generated from a snapshot. After attendance edits, OT approvals or newly-approved TADA, those figures are silently wrong until someone clicks Regenerate. The only staleness detection — ⚠ Stale badges, "Draft — review before finalizing" — lives on `/hr/calculation` (`PayrollCalculation.jsx:299–320`). Finalize is guarded by a generic `window.confirm`.
- **Why it matters:** This is the one irreversible, money-moving action in the module, and the workflow that produces the error (approve OT → return to Payroll → remember to Regenerate → Finalize) is enforced only by a footnote at `PayrollRun.jsx:499`. Stale numbers become locked payslips.
- **Fix:** Run the same live-vs-stored comparison on Payroll Run (helpers are already shared via `payrollData.js`); block or hard-warn Finalize when any slip is stale or any employee is missing, and replace the confirm with a summary dialog naming employee count, net total, TADA claims to be marked paid and advance rows to be written.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The SSF gate: copy and code disagree, and the gap can hide deducted money from the filing**
- **What:** `PayrollRun.jsx:414,499` states "SSF is applied only to employees with an SSF number," but `computePayslip` gates on the `ssf_enrolled` flag alone. An enrolled employee with a blank SSF No. is deducted 11% — and `HrReports.jsx:140` then *excludes* exactly those employees from the challan.
- **Why it matters:** Money leaves an employee's pay and does not appear on the sheet used to file it. The only signal is a quiet "N employees without an SSF number excluded" line at `HrReports.jsx:405`. This is the accountant persona's core promise — trust the exact figure — failing silently.
- **Fix:** Decide whether the number is required (logic change: gate on both, and block finalize with a clear message) or optional (copy change everywhere, plus surfacing the excluded set prominently on the challan tab). This one needs your answer before it can be fixed — see the questions below.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The label-association sweep provably never reached HR — 44 unlabeled controls measured live**
- **What:** Assessment A counted ~105 sibling `<label>`s with **zero** `htmlFor` across 12 files (`EmployeeForm.jsx` 39/0, `Advances.jsx` 10/0, `PayForm.jsx` 9/0, `TadaClaims.jsx` 9/0, `HrStaff.jsx` 9/0); only `SelfServiceHome.jsx` (11/11) is wired. Assessment B independently measured **44 unlabeled controls** on the rendered pages — Attendance 27, Settlement 7, Leave 5, plus the top-right period `select` on every single page.
- **Why it matters:** Two agents reached the same conclusion from opposite directions, and the cause matters more than the count: **HR pages use a local `lbl` style object instead of `.form-field`**, so S569's file list — built by grepping for `.form-field` — could not see them. CLAUDE.md currently claims this sweep is app-wide. It isn't, and the doc is now wrong.
- **Fix:** Wire `id`/`htmlFor` across the 12 files, and correct the CLAUDE.md claim to name the `.form-field`-based scope of what was actually swept.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] Eleven hand-rolled modal overlays; the shared `Modal.js` is used once**
- **What:** Advances, EmployeeForm, EmployeeList, HolidayCalendar, IncentiveConfigs, Overtime, PayForm, PayrollRun's PayslipModal, HrStaff (×3) and TadaClaims (×3) each hand-roll a `position:fixed; inset:0` dialog. None carries `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`, none traps Tab, most have no Escape handler — `HrStaff.jsx:70` hand-adds its own listener with a comment acknowledging the gap.
- **Why it matters:** S521 fixed dialog semantics once in `Modal.js` specifically so one fix would cover every call site. HR never adopted it, so the employee master, pay setup and payslip viewer are all unreachable-by-keyboard dialogs. B could not measure these (read-only rules kept modals closed), so the live count is likely worse than the source count suggests.
- **Fix:** Migrate the eleven to `Modal.js`. Its API takes the panel content, so most are a wrapper swap rather than a rewrite.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The contrast-variant system has zero HR adoption — and the two agents disagree about whether it matters**
- **What:** A counted **139 sites** of `color: 'var(--theme-red|green|amber|purple)'` across 25 files (worst: `PayForm.jsx` 15, `HrReports.jsx` 13, `AttendanceSheet.jsx` 10) with **zero** uses of any `*-text` variant or `accent-ink`. B measured 912 text elements live and found **zero** contrast failures outside `/hr/roster`.
- **Why it matters:** **The disagreement is the finding, and it repeats phase 1's lesson exactly.** B measured on the **dark** theme (`body` = `rgb(15,17,23)`), where base signal tokens are fine. The `*-text` variants exist because those same tokens fail AA on the five **light** presets — DESIGN.md records the worst as Rosé Dawn at 2.05:1. So the risk is real but invisible to B's evidence, and it lands on exactly the figures the product sells on: red deduction amounts, green net pay, amber warnings.
- **Fix:** Sweep the 139 text-role sites to the `*-text` variants, then verify by re-measuring on Rosé Dawn rather than dark. Mind DESIGN.md's ternary trap — most sites are `x > 0 ? red : text2`, which a property-level regex silently skips.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P2] Three copy statements contradict what the code does, all on pages that decide pay**
- `AttendanceSheet.jsx:648` — "Every day defaults to Present until you mark it otherwise." Untouched cells are deliberately not saved at all, and for daily/hourly staff an untouched day pays **zero** — the opposite.
- `LeaveManagement.jsx:360` — "Rejecting or cancelling an approved request reverts those attendance days to Present." `revertAttendance` (:126–135) deletes the rows to blank, with a comment explaining why writing Present would be fabrication.
- `PayrollRun.jsx:414` — the SSF sentence above.
- **Fix:** Correct all three to describe actual behaviour. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Two figures that don't mean what they say**
- `PayslipBody.jsx:56–61` labels the dock "Absence / Unpaid Leave (X days)" from `slip.absent_days`, which `payrollCompute.js:93` sets to absences **only** — while `absence_deduction` (:139) covers absences + unpaid leave + half-days + pre-join days. An employee with 1 absence and 3 unpaid-leave days reads "(1.0 days)" against four days of money, on the one document whose day count exists to be audited.
- `AttendanceSheet.jsx:961,990` — the Month Summary "Total" column computes P + A + O + OT, adding **days to hours**, and the tooltip confirms it. The number is dimensionally meaningless.
- **Fix:** Print the full unpaid-day count on the payslip; drop the Total column or make it days-only with OT shown separately. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Final Settlement is a calculator that leaves every consequence to memory**
- **What:** `FinalSettlement.jsx` persists nothing. After paying out, the operator must separately mark the employee inactive, settle recovered advances (otherwise `buildAdvanceMap` deducts them again on a future run — a live double-recovery path), and record leave as encashed. Nothing on the page says so. Its picker also silently excludes daily/hourly staff (:201) while `GratuityTracker.jsx:178,268` promises wage-worker gratuity "is computed at final settlement."
- **Fix:** At minimum a post-calculation checklist and a note explaining the monthly-only picker; ideally the writes themselves. Needs your call — see questions. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Measured roster contrast and touch-target failures**
- **What:** The only measured contrast failures in the module, all on `/hr/roster`: the empty-cell "+" glyph at **1.27:1** (`rgb(42,47,61)` on `rgb(24,28,39)`, 7 instances — effectively invisible); "Morning" chip text at **3.92:1** and "OFF DAY" at **4.17:1**, both 11px/700 using detector-flagged undocumented hexes as fill *and* text — the exact single-value pattern S550/S551 documents. Separately at 768px: every `.tab-btn` renders at 28px height and row checkboxes at 13px, below the 32px touch floor, on a product used from tablets; three sidebar controls (20–25px) recur app-wide, not just in HR.
- **Fix:** Split the shift-colour constants into fill vs text values; raise the "+" glyph to a visible token; extend the existing `@media (pointer: coarse)` block to cover `.tab-btn` and table checkboxes. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sabina (restaurant owner, non-technical — primary persona).** Opens Payroll after approving three OT requests; the run shows the pre-approval numbers with nothing saying so, and Finalize asks only "Are you sure?" — she locks the wrong month. On Pay Setup she meets a "CIT / Provident Fund" quick-chip with no tooltip anywhere in the module. On Attendance she reads "every day defaults to Present," leaves a daily-wage cook's row untouched, and pays them nothing. Her warnings on Pay Setup and Reports are rendered in the accent colour, which is **blue** on the Bright preset — they read as links, not cautions.

**Dipesh (accountant/bookkeeper — secondary persona, verifies rather than decides).** Reconciles SSF: eleven percent was deducted from an enrolled employee whose SSF number is blank, and that employee is missing from the challan sheet, with a single quiet line at the bottom as the only clue. Cross-checks a payslip: the absence dock says 1.0 days but the arithmetic is four. Prints a TDS certificate: the employer PAN prints as a blank line to hand-write (`HrReports.jsx:561`) even though `settings.vat_number` is already fetched elsewhere for exactly that purpose. He gets the formula transparency he needs and then loses trust on the reconciliation.

**Maya (screen-reader user, HR admin).** Tabs into Attendance and meets 27 unnamed edit boxes — every status select, time field, break, OT and note input. Opens the employee editor: a hand-rolled overlay with no `role="dialog"`, no focus trap, no Escape, so focus stays behind the modal in the page underneath. Saves a payroll run: the success or failure message renders as a coloured `<span>` in no live region, so she is not told whether the most consequential action in the product succeeded. Focus rings themselves are intact everywhere B tested — the gap is naming and announcement, not visibility.
